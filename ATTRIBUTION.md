# Data attribution / 数据署名

The map contains data derived from OpenStreetMap, available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Building footprints are derived from the Overture Maps Foundation buildings theme, ODbL 1.0. Upstream contributors represented in the selected release include OpenStreetMap contributors, Esri Community Maps contributors, Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints, Google Open Buildings, and Qian Shi et al. East Asian Buildings (CC BY 4.0).

Ocean-depth shading uses GEBCO Compilation Group (2024), GEBCO 2024 Grid (doi:10.5285/1c44ce99-0a0d-5f4f-e063-7086abc0ea0f), placed in the public domain under the [GEBCO Terms of Use](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry/terms-of-use). OSM remains authoritative for water extent.

Population, employment, education, airport, and administrative control totals are derived from the publicly available official Shanghai sources identified in `DATA_SOURCES.md`, `SOURCE-REGISTRY.json`, and `OFFICIAL-POPULATION-SOURCES.json` in the release ZIP.

本地图包含基于 OpenStreetMap 和 Overture Maps Foundation 的开放数据；海洋深度着色使用须保留来源说明的公有领域 GEBCO 2024 Grid。人口、就业、教育、机场和行政控制量来自发布包来源登记中列明的上海市及各区公开官方资料。
